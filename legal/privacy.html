Privacy Policy: Archangel Vault AI

App: Archangel Vault AI · Package: com.archangel.vaultai · Owner: Michael Glenn James Manning · Contact: TopazArchangel@gmail.com · Version: Wave 1 draft, 27 Oct 2026

1. Who we are

Archangel Vault AI ("the App") is owned and operated by Michael Glenn James Manning ("we", "us"). Contact: TopazArchangel@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Data you store in the Vault: notes, files, passwords, documents and PDFs. By default this data is stored encrypted on your device.
  • Account data: your name and email address, if you create an account.
  • AI requests: prompts you send to Ultra Intelligence AI and to Generation Studio. These go to the AI provider whose API key you configure.
  • Purchase data: subscriptions and credit purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your full card details.
  • Diagnostics: optional crash reports and basic usage data. You can opt out.

3. What we do not collect

We do not sell your data. We do not read your Vault contents. We do not use your data for advertising profiling.

4. Security

  • Data is encrypted with Android Keystore-backed keys.
  • The Vault can be unlocked with biometrics (BiometricPrompt).
  • API keys are encrypted at rest and never hardcoded.
  • Data is sent over TLS.

5. Backup, sync and cloud transfer

These features are optional and off by default. When you enable them, your data is encrypted before it is uploaded to the cloud destination you choose. That provider's own privacy policy also applies.

6. Third parties

  • Google Play Billing
  • The AI provider you configure
  • The cloud storage provider you choose

7. Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the App removes local data. To request deletion of account or cloud data, email TopazArchangel@gmail.com. We will act within 30 days.

8. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, and withdraw consent. These rights apply under the Australian Privacy Act 1988, GDPR and CCPA, where applicable.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.

10. Changes

We will notify you of material changes to this policy in the App.
